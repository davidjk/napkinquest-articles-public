What does a million tokens cost a frontier AI lab? About $154 on a lab cash basis. $253 fully loaded with capex.

Inference is $7. Capex amortization is $103, two-thirds of the stack. The popular "GPU bill" framing is off by an order of magnitude.

The $200B compute commitments price in a specific demand picture: the industry needs to roughly double its served tokens. That's about 40M paying seats at heavy-use intensity (California's population), or 1.7M devs using AI the way a Claude Code user does today.

The math closes if demand compounds. If it stalls, two of three labs survive the consolidation. The bear case strengthens if power and grid lead times choke supply faster than demand stalls.
