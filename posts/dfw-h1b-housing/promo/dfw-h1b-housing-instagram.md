  Did a $100,000 H-1B visa fee sink North Texas home prices?

  Half of that story holds up. Home-purchase mortgages to Asian Indian borrowers in Collin County fell 43% from 2023 to 2025. But the drop began in 2024, a year before the fee took effect, and only in the Sun Belt counties where these buyers had surged.

  In 2025 the drop spread to all twelve counties studied, coastal tech hubs included, while other buyers held steady. It tracked H-1B exposure: Asian Indian purchases fell 28% and Chinese purchases 13%, and India-born and China-born workers received 71% and about 12% of H-1B approvals.

  That points to tighter H-1B policy in 2025, with the fee as one part of it. The fee was in effect for only the last 102 days of 2025, and Collin's 36% drop that year was bigger than the fee alone could produce.

  The mortgage data leaves out cash buyers and doesn't record visa status, and monthly deed records would show how much of the 2025 drop came after the fee.
